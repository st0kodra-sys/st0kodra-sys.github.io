Compact pellet extruder for sustainable fused granular fabrication

PhD research, UMass Lowell, 2022–2026. Funded by a U.S. Army SBIR Phase II through re:3D Inc.

A screw extruder small and light enough to ride on a desktop printer gantry, printing directly from recycled pellets instead of filament. The work ran from screw design through a 3 kg/hr scale-up to a feedforward controller that corrects extrusion lag in the G-code.

Compact mixing screw design and validation

Kazmer & Kodra, Polymers 2025. doi:10.3390/polym17020215

Seven SLS-printed screw sections varied flight count, helix angle and mixing-slot geometry. Non-isothermal CFD in SolidWorks Flow Simulation narrowed the field to a double-flighted 20° helix with counter-rotating mixing slots, which was then machined in 316 stainless and instrumented for output and energy use.

31 mm scale-up and steady-state characterization

Kodra et al., Int. J. Adv. Manuf. Technol. 2026, first author. doi:10.1007/s00170-026-17774-7

A 31 mm version with four heating zones, a 20:1 planetary gearbox and an instrumented nozzle (melt pressure transducer and intrusive melt thermocouple). An 11-run factorial study across screw speed, barrel temperature and nozzle size, compared against an 829K-cell CFD model, showed that throughput and melt temperature can be controlled almost independently.

Transient characterization and feedforward control

Kodra et al., J. Manuf. Mater. Process. 2026, first author. doi:10.3390/jmmp10080263

Pellet extruders lag behind the motion system, so roads start thin. I logged melt pressure, melt temperature and screw position at 100 Hz with a Python DAQ, measured printed road width from 600 DPI flatbed scans in MATLAB, and identified a first-order model of the extruder. The resulting compensator runs as a G-code post-processor, with no firmware changes.

ARME: ram extruders for printing thermoset rubber

M.S. research, UMass Lowell, 2019–2022. NSF SHAP3D I/UCRC, grant IIP-1822147.

Fully compounded thermoset rubber is far too viscous for a filament extruder. The Additive Ram Material Extruder pushes it out of a heated barrel with a geared, lead-screw-driven piston, mounted on a modified desktop printer. I fabricated the first version and independently designed and built the next generations, including mechanics, electronics and firmware.

Inkbit 3D: DfAM and failure analysis

Materials R&D Associate 2022–2023, Application Engineer 2023–present. Medford, MA. Vision-controlled multi-material jetting.

As the application engineer I take client parts from inquiry to delivery: converting injection-molded designs for multi-material jetting, designing mechanical interlocks between rigid and elastomeric materials, writing proposals and running print-and-test validation before production. In situ 3D-DIC of epoxy cure

Directed study with Dr. Marianna Maiaru, UMass Lowell, fall 2021. Hexion EPON 862 / Epikure 3234.

Can stereo DIC capture strain building up around a fiber while the matrix cures? A 10 mm carbon rod was cast in epoxy and imaged every 30 s for two to three hours while thermocouples logged resin and ambient temperature.

Seven experiments across three container designs removed boundary effects and speckle failures. A MATLAB pipeline pulled peak major strain from each DIC stage and synced it to the temperature log.
